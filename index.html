RMC Price Tag Maker

Enter up to six tags, then print one sheet on letter-size cardstock.

Brand names print in uppercase. Details automatically use sentence case (first letter uppercase, everything else lowercase). Longer details wrap onto two lines and shrink to fit. Blank tags stay blank. Entries are saved automatically on this device. For best results, print at 100% scale on US Letter paper with headers and footers disabled. The exact Impact font requires Impact to be available on the device used for printing.
